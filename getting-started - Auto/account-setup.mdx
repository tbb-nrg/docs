Before you can access measurement data or configure hardware in NRG Cloud, you need an active account. This page walks you through registration, email verification, profile configuration, and team management so you're ready to start working the moment you log in.

## Account Types

NRG Cloud supports two account models depending on how your team is structured.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Individual Account" icon="user">
    Ideal for consultants or single users. You own your sites and data, and you can later join or create an organization if your needs change.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Organization Account" icon="building">
    Designed for teams sharing access to multiple sites and devices. An organization admin manages members, roles, and permissions centrally.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  If your company already has an NRG Cloud organization, ask your administrator to invite you rather than creating a standalone account. Joining through an invitation links you to your team's existing sites and data automatically.
</Note>

## Create Your Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Sign-Up Page">
    Open [cloud.nrgsystems.com](https://cloud.nrgsystems.com) in your browser and click **Sign Up** on the login screen.

    <Tip>
      NRG Cloud works best in the latest version of Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari. Make sure your browser is up to date before registering.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Details">
    Fill in your **first name**, **last name**, **email address**, and a strong **password**. Your email address becomes your login username, so use one you check regularly.

    Password requirements:

    - At least 8 characters
    - At least one uppercase letter
    - At least one number or special character
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agree to Terms and Submit">
    Review the NRG Systems Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, check the acceptance box, then click **Create Account**. NRG Cloud will immediately send a verification email to the address you provided.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Your Email Address">
    Open the verification email from NRG Cloud and click **Confirm Email**. This link expires after 24 hours — if it expires, return to the login page and request a new verification email.

    <Warning>
      You cannot log in or access any data until your email address is verified. Check your spam or junk folder if the email does not appear within a few minutes.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log In for the First Time">
    After confirming your email, return to [cloud.nrgsystems.com](https://cloud.nrgsystems.com) and sign in with your credentials. You will be prompted to complete your profile before reaching the dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Complete Your Profile

A complete profile ensures that your data timestamps, notifications, and team interactions work correctly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set Your Display Name and Organization">
    Enter the name you want shown throughout NRG Cloud and the name of your company or organization. This information appears in shared reports and team views.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Time Zone">
    Select your local time zone from the dropdown. NRG Cloud stores all measurements in UTC internally but displays times in your chosen zone across the dashboard, data views, and exports.

    <Note>
      You can override the time zone on individual sites if your measurement locations span multiple regions.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Your Profile">
    Click **Save Changes**. Your profile is now complete and you will land on the main dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Invite Team Members

Organization administrators can add colleagues directly from the **Settings → Team** page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team Settings">
    In the left navigation sidebar, click **Settings**, then select the **Team** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send an Invitation">
    Click **Invite Member**, enter the colleague's email address, and assign a role:

    | Role | Permissions |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Viewer** | Read-only access to sites and data |
    | **Operator** | View data, download files, and configure devices |
    | **Admin** | Full access, including team management and billing |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the Invitation">
    Click **Send Invite**. Your colleague will receive an email prompting them to accept the invitation and create or link their NRG Cloud account. They will appear in the **Pending** list until they accept.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can resend or revoke a pending invitation at any time from the **Team** settings page.
</Tip>

## Security and Authentication

NRG Cloud protects every account with industry-standard security measures.

- All passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.
- Sessions are protected with encrypted tokens that expire automatically after a period of inactivity.
- You can enable **multi-factor authentication (MFA)** from **Settings → Security** for an additional layer of protection.
- Single sign-on (SSO) integration is available for enterprise organizations — contact [NRG Systems support](https://www.nrgsystems.com/support) for details.

<Info>
  For enterprise or volume licensing inquiries, reach out directly to NRG Systems rather than registering through the self-service flow. Visit [nrgsystems.com](https://www.nrgsystems.com) or email your NRG account representative.
</Info>
