The NRG Cloud dashboard is your home base for everything happening across your measurement network. As soon as you log in, you see a live snapshot of your sites, recent data activity, and the health of every connected device — giving you immediate situational awareness without needing to dig through menus. This page explains each part of the interface so you can move around with confidence from day one.

## Dashboard at a Glance

When you first land on the dashboard after logging in, the main panel shows three high-level areas:

- **Sites List** — All measurement sites associated with your account or organization, with their current status and last data-received timestamp.
- **Device Status Summaries** — A quick-view of each paired hardware device (LOGR logger, ZX 300e Lidar, etc.) showing connection state, battery level, and any active alerts.
- **Recent Activity Feed** — A time-ordered log of the latest events across your sites: data uploads, configuration changes, alert triggers, and user actions.

<Note>
  The dashboard reflects data from all sites you have permission to view. If you belong to an organization, you may see sites owned by other team members depending on your role.
</Note>

## Navigation Sidebar

The left-hand sidebar is your primary way to move between sections of NRG Cloud. Each entry in the sidebar corresponds to a major functional area.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Data" icon="chart-line">
    View, filter, and download measurement records from any of your sites. Drill into individual channels, apply date ranges, and export files in your preferred format (CSV, JSON, and others).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Equipment" icon="server">
    Manage your hardware inventory, pair new devices to sites, push firmware updates, and remotely configure LOGR loggers and ZX 300e Lidars.
  </Card>
  <Card title="API" icon="code">
    Generate and manage API keys, explore available REST endpoints, and find code examples for connecting NRG Cloud to your own tools or the **nrgpy** Python library.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Settings" icon="gear">
    Update your profile, manage notification preferences, configure your organization and team members, and adjust security settings such as MFA.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  You can collapse the sidebar by clicking the **« collapse** icon at the bottom of the panel to give data views more horizontal space on smaller screens.
</Tip>

## Switching Between Sites

If your account includes multiple measurement sites, you can switch context directly from the dashboard without returning to a separate sites list.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Site Selector">
    Click the site name displayed in the top-left area of the main content panel (just below the top navigation bar). A dropdown menu lists all sites available to your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Site">
    Click the site you want to view. The dashboard, data views, and device panels all reload to show information for that site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pin Frequently Used Sites">
    Hover over a site name in the dropdown and click the **pin** icon to add it to your favourites. Pinned sites appear at the top of the list for faster access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your selected site persists across your browser session. When you log back in, NRG Cloud returns you to the last site you were viewing.
</Note>

## Notifications and Alerts Panel

The bell icon (🔔) in the top-right corner of the navigation bar opens the **Notifications Panel**. Here you can see:

- **Active alerts** — Device offline warnings, low battery notifications, data gap detections, and sensor fault flags.
- **System messages** — Platform maintenance announcements, firmware update availability, and account-level notices.
- **Resolved alerts** — A history of past alerts and when they were cleared, useful for auditing intermittent issues.

Click any notification to jump directly to the relevant site, device, or data view for more detail.

<Warning>
  Unread alerts are also surfaced as coloured status indicators on the Sites List and Device Status panels. A red indicator means the alert requires your attention; amber means a warning condition is present but not critical.
</Warning>

To configure which events generate alerts and how you receive them (email, in-app, or both), go to **Settings → Notifications**.

## Quick-Action Links

Common tasks are reachable directly from the dashboard without navigating through the full sidebar. Each site card on the Sites List panel includes shortcut buttons:

| Quick Action | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **View Data** | Opens the data explorer filtered to that site's most recent records |
| **Download Files** | Opens the download dialog pre-set for the selected site |
| **Configure Device** | Jumps to the equipment configuration screen for the site's primary device |
| **View Alerts** | Filters the Notifications Panel to show only alerts for that site |

## Display Preferences

You can adjust how NRG Cloud renders information to match your working style.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Display Settings">
    Click your avatar or initials in the top-right corner of the navigation bar, then select **Display Preferences** from the dropdown menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Colour Theme">
    Toggle between **Light** and **Dark** mode. Your preference is saved to your account and applies across all browsers and devices where you are signed in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Your Default Time Zone Display">
    Choose whether timestamps across the dashboard appear in your **profile time zone**, the **site's local time zone**, or **UTC**. This setting does not change how data is stored — it only affects how times are labelled in the interface.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Your Preferences">
    Click **Save** to apply your changes immediately. The dashboard reloads with your new display settings active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Display preferences are user-specific. Changing your theme or time zone display does not affect how other team members see the dashboard.
</Info>
